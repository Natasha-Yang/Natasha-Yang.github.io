I’m a 4th-year Engineering Science student specializing in Machine Intelligence at the University of Toronto. My research interests span robot learning, reinforcement learning, and generative modeling, with a focus on enabling robots to acquire new capabilities with limited experience.

Currently, I work with [Prof. Florian Shkurti](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~florian/) on inference-time adaptation of pretrained robot policies. Previously, at RBC Capital Markets, I developed RL algorithms and simulation infrastructure for [Aiden](https://rbcborealis.com/research-blogs/aiden-reinforcement-learning-for-order-execution/), an electronic trading platform. I have also worked with [Prof. Eitan Grinspun](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~eitan/) on graphic media adaptation and [Prof. Michael Brudno](https://brudno.uhndata.io/) on semi-supervised medical image segmentation.

I’m always happy to connect with people who share similar research interests!
